## Part 1: omitted terms along the unchanged filter

Each case is replayed from its start row (dt = 0 there) with its stored initial SoC, and the estimate and prior voltage equal the stored trace in `results/runs/3rcd0/` on every row. Experiment 1 is measured on all 63 runs over the whole run (full) and over the evaluation window; the 30 wrong-start cases of guard 7 (decision 1.48: peak above 25 pp in experiment 2, 3 low or 3 high) over the first 30 s after their start. Current, temperature and the stored OCV slope are held as in S10 (`results/s10/accuracy_diagnostics.md`): the SoC derivatives are those of the clamped bilinear tables, zero outside the SoC axis; H is the omitted -I dR0/dSoC at the prior SoC and the row's own current; its ratio to the stored OCV slope uses the rows where that slope is at least 0.05 V per unit SoC; F1 to F3 are the omitted SoC column of F per branch at the previous posterior state and the held current. The covariance ratio and the normalised cross change compare F_full P F_full^T with F P F^T at the recorded posterior P and Q, as in S10. The correction change is |(K_full - K_legacy)_SoC| times the absolute innovation, in pp per row: K_legacy is Eq. 9 with the filter's prior covariance and H, K_full with F_full P F_full^T + Q and the full H.

Each entry is the largest run-level value in its split, temperature and scope: the 95th percentile, or the sum over the scope for the last column. Support is summed. Per-run values are in [jacobian_3rcd0.csv](jacobian_3rcd0.csv). These are local sensitivities: a one-step change in P or K does not bound the cumulative effect of a changed filter in either direction.
