The ratio uses rows with absolute stored slope at least 0.05 V per unit SoC. The active cell starts at each interior SoC knot in grid order, the final knot uses the last cell, and outside the grid the SoC derivative is zero. Current and temperature are held fixed in the partial derivatives. The covariance comparison is one step at the same posterior P and Q. The Frobenius ratio mixes SoC and voltage units. The final column normalizes the largest absolute SoC-to-branch covariance change by the corresponding legacy prior standard deviations.
