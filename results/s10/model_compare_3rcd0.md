# 3RC-D0 comparison (S10)

Generated by `make ekf-3rcd0` under decision 1.44.

The 23 standard runs are a previously inspected regression suite. They informed earlier diagnostics and the low-SoC boundary choice. They share segments with the 40 Mix runs, and the Mix folds also share segments with each other. All runs come from one cell in one campaign.

All filters use frozen S2 noise and legacy Jacobians. Only 3RC-D0 versus 2RC-I enters adoption. The S9 R1 substitution retains the original S9 R2/R3 coefficients and uses v1.0 R1 for both signs.

The [fit report](model_fit_3rcd0.md) compares open-loop voltage for all five models on both splits.
