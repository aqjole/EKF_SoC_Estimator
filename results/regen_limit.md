# Regen rows at the 4.2 V limit (S11 step B diagnostic)

Generated by `make diagnose-regen`; decisions 1.48 and 1.51.

The 23 standard runs are a previously inspected regression suite. They informed earlier diagnostics and the low-SoC boundary choice. They share segments with the 40 Mix runs, and the Mix folds also share segments with each other. All runs come from one cell in one campaign.

A limit row is a row at or above 4.1995 V whose own current is below zero. A limit interval is a maximal set of limit rows in which consecutive limit rows are at most 1 s apart; the rows between them belong to the interval. The change across an interval is the experiment-1 SoC error at its last row minus the error at the row before it, from the stored traces, so it includes the filter's response to every row inside the interval. 3RC-D0-N is 3RC-D0 under the S11 step A candidate settings (1.51), which were not adopted.
