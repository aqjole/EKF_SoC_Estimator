# EKF tuning

Written by `python/tune.py` (`make tune`). Do not edit by hand.

The noise settings q_soc, q_v and r of the filter (docs/model.md Eq. 7 and Eq. 9) are tuned once, on the tuning cycle only (docs/decisions.md 1.3), and then frozen (docs/decisions.md 1.15).

## Method

- Tuning cycle: `25degC/10-29-18_14.41 551_Mixed1_25degC_LGHG2.mat`, 77226 rows used, 68794 of them in the evaluation window (6879.3 s).
- Objective: the SoC RMSE in pp of experiment 2 over the evaluation window (docs/model.md C14): the filter starts at the first row with the initial SoC 20 pp below the reference, V1 = V2 = 0, and is not told the init is wrong.
- Fixed: P0 = diag(0.2^2, 0.05^2, 0.05^2); the 2RC tables of `results/params.csv`.
- Grid: one point per decade, q_soc 1e-10 to 1e-5 1/s, q_v 1e-8 to 1e-3 V^2/s, r 1e-6 to 1e-1 V^2: 6 x 6 x 6 = 216 points.
- Refinement: the 3 x 3 x 3 = 27 points at factors 1/3, 1 and 3 of the best grid point, each value rounded to 3 significant digits. The refinement may leave the grid.
- Ties go to the point that comes first in the order of evaluation (q_soc slowest, r fastest).
