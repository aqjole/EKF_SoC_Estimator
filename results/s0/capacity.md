# Capacity tests (S0)

Generated by `python/inventory.py` (`make inventory`) from `data/raw/` (LG 18650HG2, DOI 10.17632/b5mj79w5w9.2, CC BY 4.0). Do not edit by hand. Every number is measured from the files.

## Method

- The discharge of a test is the longest run of samples with current below -50 mA (cycler sign). For C20DisCh this is the C/20 discharge part; its charge part is reported separately.
- *Ah removed* = Ah counter at the start of the discharge minus Ah counter at its last sample. The cycler counts discharge as negative Ah, so this is positive.
- *Ah at start*: the counter at the rest sample just before the discharge. Files that open mid-discharge (last column) are extrapolated back one logging interval at the first sample's current; check: for the 12 C/20 and 2C files this gives |Ah at start| <= 0.00004 Ah (counter reset at the step start); for the 6 0.5C files it gives the last Ah value of that folder's C20DisCh file to within 0.00007 Ah (counter not reset).
- *final V*: voltage at the last discharge sample. *mean I*: mean of the logged current over the discharge, sign flipped so discharge is positive (the *Anomalies* section uses the median instead). *cell T*: Battery_Temp_degC min / max over the discharge.
- Rows are in campaign order (first TimeStamp of each file), so ageing across the campaign shows down the table. Folder order by first TimeStamp: 25degC, 40degC, 10degC, 0degC, n10degC, n20degC. The readme gives the order 40, 25, 10, 0, -10, -20 degC.

## Anomalies

- **10degC test 575: currents differ from the other folders.** Median discharge current: C/20 0.148 A (other folders 0.153 A, ratio 0.967), 0.5C 1.451 A (other folders 1.499 A, ratio 0.968), 2C 5.800 A (other folders 6.000 A, ratio 0.967). HPPC discharge pulses: -2.9 / -5.8 / -11.6 / -17.4 A, against -3.0 / -6.0 / -12.0 / -18.0 A at 25degC (ratio 0.967 for every level). Median of these ratios: 0.967. Against the 3.0 A 1C basis, the 10degC 0.5C and 2C capacities were therefore measured at 0.48C and 1.93C. The folder's 1C tests ran at the usual current (mean I: 3.000 A in 571, 3.000 A in 576).
- **C/20 charge / discharge Ah ratio**, in campaign order: 1.068 (25degC), 1.066 (40degC), 1.018 (10degC), 1.034 (0degC), 1.015 (n10degC), 0.983 (n20degC). With no charge lost or created the ratio would be 1, or just above 1 for a coulombic efficiency under 1 (more charge goes in than comes out). The measured ratios above 1 (1.015 to 1.068) are far larger than that, and n20degC is below 1 (0.983). The data explains none of them, so all are recorded as unexplained, and the two halves of a C20DisCh file should not be assumed to share one Ah axis.
- **Drive-cycle stop rule (95 % of the 1C capacity): partly.** In all 42 drive cycles that share a cycler test number with a 1C test, Ah removed / that 1C capacity is 0.9500 to 0.9501, even where that 1C test ran off-temperature (tests 557, 571). Groups without a same-number 1C test are compared with the first 1C test that ran at the folder temperature. Do not fit: 567 (10degC) removed 97.4 % of 576's 1C capacity; 582 (10degC) removed 94.8 % of 576's 1C capacity; 590 (0degC) removed 94.1 % of 589's 1C capacity; 601 (n10degC) removed 94.1 % of 596's 1C capacity; 604 (n10degC) removed 93.2 % of 596's 1C capacity. Cannot be checked (no 1C test at the folder temperature): 556 (40degC). Partial runs: 562 (40degC: mixed4, mixed5, mixed6, mixed7, mixed8) removed only 0.19 Ah per cycle.
